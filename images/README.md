## Extra figures (NOT in the report)

Per the assignment, additional generated images are kept in `extras/` with
their significance documented here:

- **`extras/C08_ratio_boxes.png`** — Upvote-ratio box plots for the six most
  contested and six most unanimous subreddits. Explored during Task T2; not
  included in the report because consensus contrasts are already visible in
  Figures 6 (fingerprint column) and 12 (ratio-vs-comments relationship).
- **`extras/C12_corr_matrix.png`** — Spearman correlation matrix of the
  post-level variables. Used during exploration to select which relationships
  to pursue (score↔comments, ratio↔comments, crossposts↔score); the numbers
  it contains are cited directly in the report text (Sections 5.3 and 6)
  instead of the figure.
- **`extras/C15_crossposts.png`** — Crossposts vs. score scatter (ρ = 0.65),
  with the most-crossposted posts labelled. Supports finding F7
  ("media travels; text stays home"), which is reported numerically in the
  report rather than as a figure, to keep the narrative focused.
